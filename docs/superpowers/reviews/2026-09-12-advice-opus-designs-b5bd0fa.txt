1. **BLOCKING — `specs/2026-09-11-birth-cohort-attribution-design.md:62`. The refutation is invalid; the probe measured a future window.** BSD `find -newerBt` parses its timestamp in **local time**. The probe pasted the event's UTC strings (`"2026-09-11 23:37:52"` / `"2026-09-12 00:07:40"`) into it, so the window it actually searched was `2026-09-12T06:37:52Z–07:07:40Z` — later than the probe's own execution time. Zero matches was arithmetically guaranteed, not an empirical negative. The "validity control" doesn't rescue it: a single-sided 24h `-newerBt` is insensitive to a 7-hour offset, so it validated a different query than the one that returned 0. I verified the local-time parsing empirically (a file born now matches a local-string window and misses the identical UTC-string window). The correct local window is `2026-09-11 16:37:52` .. `17:07:40`. Re-running the probe at that window, 13 hours after the event (so deletions since are already excluded — this is a lower bound):

   | root | files | newborn GiB |
   |---|---|---|
   | `/private/tmp` | 84,770 | **3.74** |
   | `$TMPDIR/T` | 378 | 0.01 |
   | `~/.cache` | 1 | 0.00 |
   | `~/roadmap/.../evidence` | 0 | 0.00 |

   ≈21% of the 18.03 GiB delta still on disk half a day later, under `/private/tmp` — a root whose `hot_dirs_kb` was null, which is the second half of the stated success bar. **Fix:** do not close `disk_magician-6wd` as refuted. Correct the spec's §3–§5, re-run the probe promptly against a fresh step event with local-time (or `-newerBt` with an explicit TZ) bounds, and re-judge against the ≥30% bar. 3. **BLOCKING — `specs/2026-09-11-cleanup-tmp-single-lsof-design.md:219-224`.
