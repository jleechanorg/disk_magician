## New capability

`scripts/slim_worktree_checkouts.sh` + `diskm slim-worktrees`
(`tests/test_slim_worktree_checkouts.sh`, 18 assertions, all passing): excludes
heavy tracked dirs from dormant worktrees with per-worktree sparse-checkout,
leaving git history intact and reversible with `git sparse-checkout disable`.
Gates: 3-day recency floor, live-cwd, AO-owned (including an AO-config
`worktreeDir` match), `safety_gate`, and skip-if-local-changes-under-excluded-path.
Dry-run by default; `--clean` needs `WORKTREE_APPROVED=1`.

A real bug was caught by the dry run before any damage: the AO guard pattern
`*/ao/data/worktrees/*` does not match `.ao/data/worktrees/`, so the first pass
offered 591 worktrees / 73.7 GB including live AO agents. Fixed, tested, and
recorded in `findings_wiki/2026-10-22-worktree-sizing-and-sparse-docs.md`.

## Phase 2 — AO worktrees (same day, after 88% full again)

Disk refilled to 96% within hours, and the AO worktree fleet was the cause. Three
read-only lanes measured it before anything was touched:

- `~/.ao/data/worktrees`: 181 worktrees, 68.3 GiB, of which **27.3 GiB was `docs/`**;
  170 slim-eligible, 126 clean+unpushed-free, 55 keep (live/dirty/young).
- `~/.worktrees`: **582 worktrees, 231.8 GiB**; 172 clean+unpushed-free (55.4 GiB),
  70 slim-only (33.5 GiB), 340 keep; 29 live-cwd, 201 dirty.
- The AO config's *global* `worktreeDir: ~/.worktrees` had made the first version of
  `slim-worktrees` treat every worktree there as AO-owned and skip it. That is a
  default root, not a per-checkout ownership claim — fixed by running with
  `--include-ao` (the 3-day, live-cwd, dirty and safety gates still apply).

### Blocked, by design or by privilege

- Whole-worktree removal: `cleanup-worktrees` reported **0 eligible / 846 preserved**,
  every row `cwd-unknown` — the strict liveness gate fails closed because this host has
  no root cwd scanner. No worktree can be removed on Linux until that is rolled out.
  Bead filed (`linux-liveness-gate-and-colima-path`).
- `cleanup-colima` skips: it looks for `~/.colima/_lima`, the VM is at `~/.config/colima`
  (8.8 GiB, running). Same bead.
- `cleanup-tmp --large`: every `/tmp` dir is <24 h old — the box reboots and refills
  ~100 GiB/day, so an age-gated sweeper structurally reclaims nothing there.
- Needs a human: Ollama models 17 G (5 models, 8 G is one abliterated gemma3), ComfyUI
  checkpoint 6.5 G, Trash 2.1 G, journal 2.0 G + `/var/crash` 1.1 G (root).

Trap recorded: `/tmp/ao-brd-*` is a **symlink to `~/.ao`** (80 GiB); prefix-based
sweeping or `du /tmp/*/` double-counts it and could delete through it.
